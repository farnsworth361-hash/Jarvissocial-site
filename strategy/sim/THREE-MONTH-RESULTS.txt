                             READ THIS BEFORE THE NUMBERS                             
======================================================================================
Across EVERY scenario the 90% range is roughly $1,050 to $2,000.
Three months is too short for the edge — if there is one — to
separate itself from noise. Even 'filters work well' has a wide
losing tail, and even 'no edge' has winning paths. You cannot tell
these apart from three months of P&L. That is the whole point.

~17 closed trades is a quarter of the 60 that section 10
requires before the strategy can be judged at all.
