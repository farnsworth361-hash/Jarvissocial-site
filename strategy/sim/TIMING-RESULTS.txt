====================================================================================================
                                          WHAT THIS MEANS                                           
====================================================================================================
Every position resolves within ~24 trading days (~5 weeks calendar).
There is no open-ended hold: the 10-DTE rule closes it regardless.

The three exits fire on very different clocks:

  LOSERS ARE FAST   — median 7 trading days, 25th pct just 4.
  WINNERS ARE SLOW  — median 10-12 days; a target needs a sustained
                      directional move, not a single good session.
  TIME EXITS ARE RARE — only 2-3% of trades reach 10 DTE untouched.

THE MECHANISM BEHIND THE STOP RATE (the important part):

The stop fires on ~67-72% of trades, and that is mostly NOT the
underlying moving against you. It is theta.

Hold SPY perfectly FLAT at 765.14 and price the 785/790 vertical at
10 DTE: it is worth about $0.70. The stop sits at $0.725. So a trade
that simply goes nowhere decays into its own stop with days to spare.

  -> 'Stop' in this system mostly means DID NOT MOVE IN TIME,
     not MOVED AGAINST ME.

That is a deliberate property, not a defect: an OTM debit vertical
that has not moved by the halfway point has usually lost its thesis,
and cutting it at -50% frees the capital and the slot. But it means
the loss rate is high and front-loaded by construction, and the
occasional slow winner has to carry all of them at 2:1.

So the felt experience is: frequent small losses inside the first
week or two, occasional slower wins, and very little that lingers.
Judging this in week three is judging noise -- which is exactly why
section 10 requires 60 closed trades before any verdict.

NOTE ON SCOPE: these are TIMING results. Outcomes are recorded at the
barrier level, not at the actual value on the crossing day, so this
simulation should NOT be read as an expectancy estimate. Paths use
risk-neutral drift, which assumes no edge by construction.
