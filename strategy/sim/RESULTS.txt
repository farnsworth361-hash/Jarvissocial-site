The second variant is the important one. It uses disciplined stops,
never lets anything expire worthless, and has a roughly FLAT
arithmetic edge -- and it still goes to zero, because betting the
full account makes the geometric return deeply negative even when
the average trade is fine. That gap is volatility drag, and it is
what position sizing exists to defeat. It is the entire reason the
RATCHET convexity sleeve is capped at 30% and swept weekly.
