                                              READING THIS                                              
========================================================================================================
Every GAP is positive, and that is correct, not a bug: under
risk-neutral drift no configuration profits by itself. The market
prices these fairly. GAP is simply how much the entry filters must
add before a config makes money.

The trade-off is the whole answer:

  LOWER TARGET  -> resolves faster and hits far more often, but the
                   required hit rate rises much faster than the
                   achieved one. +50% needs ~60%; +100% needs ~40%.

  SHORTER DTE   -> shortens the clock, but the 10-DTE exit eats most
                   of the holding window and gamma/theta both bite
                   harder. The bar goes up, not down.

Shortening hold time is available. It is not free, and on these
numbers it is not cheap either.
