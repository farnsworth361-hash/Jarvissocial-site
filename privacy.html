Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jarvis Social ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service to connect your social media accounts and publish your own content.

1. Information we collect

  • Account connection data. When you connect a social platform (e.g., TikTok) using that platform's official login, we receive an authorization token and basic profile information you authorize, such as your display name, avatar, and account identifier.
  • Content you choose to publish. The videos, captions, and posts you direct us to publish to your connected accounts.
  • Usage data. Basic technical logs (timestamps, errors) used to operate and secure the service.

We do not collect your social account password. Authentication is handled entirely by the platform's official OAuth login.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate you with the platforms you connect.
  • To publish and schedule the content you explicitly choose, to the accounts you explicitly connect.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not post to your accounts without your explicit instruction.
  • We do not use your content for advertising or to train third-party models.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the platforms you connect (to perform the actions you request) and with infrastructure providers that help us run the service, under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain connection tokens and settings only while your account is connected. You can disconnect any platform at any time, which revokes our access and deletes the associated tokens. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

Access tokens are stored securely and transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Third-party platforms

Your use of connected platforms (such as TikTok) is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Children

Jarvis Social is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions or data requests: support@jarvissocial.net
