Jarvis Social

Publish your content, everywhere.

Jarvis Social lets creators securely connect their own social accounts and publish the content they create across platforms — from one place.

What it does

You connect your social accounts through each platform's official login. Jarvis Social then helps you schedule and publish your own videos and posts to those accounts, at your direction.

You stay in control

Jarvis Social only acts on the accounts you explicitly connect, and only to publish content you choose. You can disconnect any account at any time.
